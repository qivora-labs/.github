### How we stay accountable

- **Senior people stay on the work** — architecture and delivery stay with people who can own them, not a sales hand-off after kickoff.
- **Discovery before the expensive part** — a written plan, assumptions, and a clean path to say no before a full build starts.
- **Shipped, then handed over** — working software every sprint, with notes and pairing so your team can take the wheel when it wants.

We will not invent case studies, inflate headcount, or promise a large bench. If we do not have the right people yet, we say so.

### A process you can audit

```text
Discover → Architect → Build → Harden → Scale
```

| Phase | Cadence | You leave with |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Discover** | 1–2 weeks | Product brief, domain model, success metrics |
| **Architect** | 1–3 weeks | Prototype, ADRs, delivery plan |
| **Build** | Two-week sprints | Working increments, demos, live velocity |
| **Harden** | 2–4 weeks | Load & security reports, WCAG audit, runbooks |
| **Scale** | Ongoing | SLOs, cost tuning, structured team handover |

Sprint length is two weeks. Demos every sprint. Timezone overlap planned with you. After launch: support, a smaller pod, or a full handoff — agreed in writing.
